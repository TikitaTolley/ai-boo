# Boo Pumpkin audio tracks

| Choice | SD card path | Voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `mp3/0001.mp3` | Ghost whispering boo |
| 2 | `mp3/0002.mp3` | Demonic voice saying boo |
| 3 | `mp3/0003.mp3` | Sharp witch saying boo |

Copy the whole `mp3` folder to the root of the FAT32-formatted DFPlayer microSD card.

Use `playMp3Folder(1)`, `playMp3Folder(2)`, or `playMp3Folder(3)` in the firmware to select a voice.
