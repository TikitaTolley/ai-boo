# The Haunted Sweet Bowl

A Halloween sweet bowl that weighs its contents and plays spooky voices or a sinister laugh when someone takes sweets.

## Parts

- ESP32-C3 SuperMini
- 1 kg load cell and HX711 module
- DFPlayer Mini
- Mini speaker, 3 W, 4 ohm
- FAT32-formatted microSD card
- 5V USB supply
- Printed bowl and weighing base
- M4 screws

The load cell sits beneath the bowl with clearance to flex. The controller waits for the weight to settle before responding to a change.
